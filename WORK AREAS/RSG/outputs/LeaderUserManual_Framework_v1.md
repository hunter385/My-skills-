# The Leader User Manual

**Subtitle:** How you think, work, and react — on one page, so your team stops guessing.

*Why the name works: the appliance metaphor teaches before you say a word. Everything in the building ships with a manual except the most complex system in it — you.*

---

## THE PROBLEM THIS SOLVES

Most pastors have never put how they think, work, and react on paper — so their team can't make a single real decision without routing it back through the one person they can't clone.

---

## THE FRAMEWORK

A person on paper. Head to heart. Top to bottom.

**HEAD — Put How You Decide On Paper**
The documented version of how you weigh a call: what you value, what a win looks like, what you'd do when no one asks.
*Fixes:* the "What would the pastor do here?" guess that stalls every decision made without you in the room.

**HANDS — Put How You Work On Paper**
The documented version of your operating standard: how work gets pitched, submitted, and finished before it reaches your desk.
*Fixes:* the friction of staff doing good work the wrong way, because you finally named the rules you were enforcing in your head.

**HEART — Put How You React On Paper**
The documented version of the relationship: what lights you up, what sets you off, what to do when things go sideways.
*Fixes:* alignment problems that look like performance problems. The relationship survives the work because the unspoken rules got spoken.

---

## THE PAIRED CONTRAST

The Leader User Manual without brutal honesty leads to another values poster nobody follows.

Brutal honesty without the Leader User Manual leads to a leader the team admires — and still can't predict.

---

## THE DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION

Ask your top staff person: *"If you had to make a real decision this afternoon without checking with me — what's the first thing you'd be unsure about?"*

Whatever they say is the exact page missing from your manual.

---

## THE PROOF POINT

You know this is working when a decision gets made this week that you would have made — your call, not their version of it — and you weren't in the room when it happened.

---

## RSG FIT CHECK

**Chain link:** Role clarity feeding daily execution.

**Core truth:** "You don't need more ideas. You need a system that gets your team executing." The manual documents the operator so the operation runs without the operator. Also touches the messy-middle truth — an undocumented leader is exactly where a motivated team falls apart, because day-one energy can't replace written rules.

---

## THE PUNCH CLOSE

Stop making them guess.
