## THE HOOK *(60 seconds — say it in full)*

> "Here's the most expensive sentence on your staff:
>
> *'If I don't catch it, nobody does.'*
>
> If that's true in your building, you don't have a staffing problem the way you think you do.
>
> You think your staff has a problem-*solving* problem.
>
> They don't.
>
> They have a problem-*seeing*, problem-*owning*, and problem-*spacing* problem.
>
> Three blockers. All three fixable. That's today."

---

## THE TENSION BUILD *(3–5 min — three beats before the framework)*

**Beat 1 — Name the symptom.**

You're the smoke detector. The only one in the building.

Every problem routes to your desk to get solved.

You walk into staff meeting hoping to hear *"here's what's broken and here's what I'm already doing about it."*

Instead you get status updates. Nobody brings the hard thing.

**Beat 2 — The river.** *(Tell it slow.)*

> "You and a friend are at a picnic by a river. You hear a shout — a child is drowning. You both dive in, grab the kid, drag them to shore. Before you catch your breath, another cry. Back in. Then another child drifts by. And another. You can barely keep up.
>
> Then you see your friend climbing out, walking away.
>
> You yell, *'Where are you going?'*
>
> And your friend says: *'Upstream. To stop whoever's throwing them in.'*"

That's your staff.

Soaking wet. Pulling kids out of the river all week long. Exhausted.

And nobody has walked upstream.

Here's the trap — the person who pulls the most kids out gets praised. You're rewarding busy over fixed. The fire never gets prevented, because fighting it is what gets applauded.

**Beat 3 — Wrong diagnosis, real cause.**

Most pastors look at this and think: *I need smarter people.*

You don't.

The same staff sitting in that meeting can find and fix what's been broken for three cycles straight — the day you stop adding people and start removing blockers.

There are three. Let me name them.

## THE FRAMEWORK *(10–15 min — Name It. Own It. Make Room.)*

**The point — if they remember one sentence:**

> *Problems don't persist because they're hard. They persist because they're blind, unowned, or buried.*

**The mechanism.** Every problem that won't die is stuck at one of three points:

- You can't fix what you can't **see**.
- You can't fix what no one will **own**.
- You can't fix what you have no **room** to touch.

Remove the specific blocker and the problem moves — without anyone getting smarter.

**The three blockers and the three moves:**

| The Blocker — why it persists | The Move — how an A-Team beats it |
|---|---|
| **1. Problem Blindness** — they don't see it, or they've accepted it as inevitable. *"Every church deals with this."* | **Name It** — say it out loud, make it real. A problem you refuse to name becomes furniture. You walk past it every day and stop seeing it. |
| **2. Lack of Ownership** — they see it, but it's *"not my department."* | **Own It** — someone volunteers. Ownership is chosen, never assigned. Nothing changes until one person says *"this is mine."* |
| **3. Tunneling** — too buried in urgent to touch it. Headlights on the ten feet ahead, blind to the road. | **Make Room** — create slack. Margin feels like waste. It's where every fix is born. |

**Order matters.** Name → Own → Make Room.

You can't own a problem you can't see. You can't make room for a problem no one owns.

**The metaphor — three locked doors.**

Every persistent problem sits behind three locked doors. A name. An owner. Room to move.

Most staffs stand at the first door with no key. That's why the problem never moves.

An A-Team walks in carrying all three.

**Draw it.** *(Whiteboard / screen — under thirty seconds.)*

```
  PROBLEM → [ BLIND ] → [ UNOWNED ] → [ BURIED ] → FIXED
                │           │             │
             Name It      Own It      Make Room
```

**The Q2 anchor.** Covey's matrix — prevention lives in Quadrant 2: important, not urgent. A tunneled staff never gets there. Slack is how you get there.

**Make it precise — what this is NOT:**

- This is *unblocking*, not solving. It gets a problem to where it *can* be solved.
- **Naming** is precise and ownable. It is NOT complaining — complaining is vague and ownerless.
- **Ownership** is volunteered responsibility. It is NOT assigning blame.
- **Slack** is reserved Q2 time. It is NOT downtime, laziness, or an empty calendar.

**The reframe — say it plain:**

Old belief: *"My staff can't solve problems."*

New belief: *"My staff is blocked, not incapable."*

The recurring problem was never hard. It was blind, unowned, or buried — for three cycles.

**The diagnostic question — ask it, let it sit:**

> "Pick one problem that's been on your list three cycles running. Which gate is it stuck at — blind, unowned, or buried?"

That question is the bridge into what we do next.

---

## THE LIVE APPLICATION *(10–15 min — they build it on the call)*

Everybody grab the worksheet. We're running one real problem all the way through. Right now.

Read these out loud, one at a time. Pause after each. Nobody leaves with a blank sheet.

> **THE STUCK PROBLEM WORKSHEET**

**Step 1 — Pick one.** One problem that's recurred for two or more cycles. Not your whole list. One.

**Step 2 — Name it in one sentence.** Specific enough to be undeniable. Add a number if you can.
*Vague:* "Our follow-up is weak." → *Named:* "We lose 3 of every 5 new volunteers in their first 60 days."

**Step 3 — Find the gate.** Circle one: Blind · Unowned · Buried. Where is it actually stuck?

**Step 4 — Why has it persisted?** One line. What's kept that door locked?

**Step 5 — Name the one owner.** A person. Not a committee. Shared ownership is no ownership.

**Step 6 — Make the room.** Put a day and time on the calendar this week to work it. Then write what you're removing to create that time. No removal, no room.

**Step 7 — The next move + date.** One concrete action in the next 7 days. The first move — not the whole fix.

**Share out:** two or three pastors read their named problem and their one owner to the group. We pressure-test the naming together.

**Where this lives after today:** start a **Stuck Problem Log** — a running list of recurring problems, same place as your cycle rock dashboard. Each Cooldown, you pull one off the list and run this sheet. Commit the cycle's problems straight into the growth plan.

---

## THE CLOSE *(2 min)*

One move before our next call.

Take the worksheet you just filled out. Put the room on the calendar — the actual block, for the actual owner. Today.

Not the fix. The room. The fix dies without it.

Don't go hunting for a smarter staff. Unblock the one you have.

Name it. Own it. Make room. The rest was never the hard part.

> **Stop fishing kids out of the river.**
>
> **Go upstream.**

## COACHING TRIGGERS *(watch for these — what to say)*

**1. "That's just ministry — every church deals with this."** *(Problem blindness, live.)*

That's the blocker talking. Steelman it, then turn it:

> "Fair — some of this *is* the cost of doing ministry. That said, 'every church deals with it' is exactly how a problem becomes furniture. Name it with a number. If you can measure it, you can decide whether it's actually unfixable — or just unnamed. Adopt that, or adapt it to your context."

**2. "My whole team should own this."** *(Ownership diffused into a committee.)*

> "When everyone owns it, no one does. I'm not asking who *cares* about it. I'm asking whose name goes on the line this cycle. One name. Who?"

**3. Goes quiet, or "I don't have time for this right now."** *(Tunneling — and they don't hear the irony.)*

> "Hear what you just said. *That's* the tunnel. You don't have time to fix the thing that's costing you time. The move isn't working harder inside the tunnel — it's making room to step out of it. What's one thing on this week's calendar you can cut?"

## SEGMENT SNAPSHOT

- **Teaching point:** Problems persist because they're blind, unowned, or buried — not because they're hard.
- **Framework:** Name It. Own It. Make Room. *(three gates: Blind → Unowned → Buried)*
- **Live move:** Each pastor runs one real stuck problem through the 7-step Stuck Problem Worksheet and blocks the calendar room before leaving.
- **Timing:** Banter shift 1 min · Hook 1 min · Tension 4 min · Framework 13 min · Live application 13 min · Close 2 min · (Triggers as needed) ≈ 34 min. Trim Beat 1 or the share-out to land at 30.
