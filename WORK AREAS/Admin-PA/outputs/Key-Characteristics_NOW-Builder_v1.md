# THE NOW BUILDER
**Topic: 3 Key Characteristics**
*One move they make before the feeling wears off*

---

## THE ACTION

**The one move in the next 24 hours:**
Write your mission in one sentence at the top of a blank page. Then brainstorm — what does your team need to accomplish it? Get 10 characteristics on paper. Don't filter. Don't rank. Just go wide.

**Small enough to do today. Big enough to matter.**
It takes 15 minutes. It's the brainstorm — and the brainstorm is what every other step runs through. Without it, nothing else can happen.

**What proves they actually started:**
A page with their mission at the top and at least 10 characteristics below it. That's proof of work.

**The move, not the intention:**
"I will write my mission and brainstorm 10 characteristics before tomorrow morning."

---

## THE NEXT

**After the first move — the second:**
Run the elimination round. Look at every characteristic on the list and ask: "Would a hospital put this on their list?" If yes, cross it off. See what survives.

**What keeps it going past day one:**
Share the ranked list with one person on your team this week. Not for approval — for a gut check. "Does this feel like us? Does anything feel missing?"

**When they check back in:**
Next coaching call. Come back with 3 characteristics — named, ranked, and one sentence for why each one is non-negotiable for your mission.

**The rep they repeat this week:**
Each day, look at one real decision or conversation. Ask: "Does this reflect Characteristic #1?" That's the install rep. Run it every day until it's automatic.

---

## THE REMEMBER

**One line out the door:**
A filter or a welcome mat — your characteristics choose.

**What they say when they explain it to someone:**
"Most churches have values that could belong to any church in America. We're building three things that are specific to our mission, ranked in order. They tell us who can be here and who can't. That's the difference between a culture that drifts and a team that executes."

**The phrase that snaps them back:**
"Filter or welcome mat?"

**Worth putting on a sticky note:**
3. Ranked. Mission-specific. Everything else is a welcome mat.
